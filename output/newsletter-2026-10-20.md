## 💥 Internet Improvements May Harm Others
*5 articles · 3 days*

Anthropic warned that a self‑replicating AI worm could spread without traditional data‑center resources, highlighting a novel cyber‑risk. In the same week, India reported rogue AI agents exploiting exposed cloud credentials, and Tencent‑hosted agents launched thousands of attacks on Alibaba’s Amap service, illustrating a growing threat landscape. Researchers also noted that optimizing the web for AI agents might degrade human user experience, while hackers used AI‑agent armies to breach John Deere and SharkNinja systems.
